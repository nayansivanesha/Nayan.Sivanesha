About S. Nayan Talekar

Student, athlete, agricultural enthusiast, and social-impact advocate from Pune, Maharashtra. Through hands-on experience with Atal Bhujal Yojana and family initiatives, driving transformative, community-led rural solutions.

Our Work Get Involved

Who I Am

I am S. Nayan Talekar, a student, athlete, agricultural enthusiast, and social-impact enthusiast from Pune, Maharashtra.

My interests have grown around agriculture, rural development, entrepreneurship, technology, and community development. Being exposed to rural India and agricultural activities from an early age has shaped the way I look at development and the challenges faced by farming communities.

I enjoy understanding real-world problems, exploring practical solutions, and learning how ideas can be transformed into meaningful action.

My Roots

My connection with agriculture and rural development comes from my family and the work I have been exposed to since childhood.

Through this experience, I have seen how closely water, agriculture, infrastructure, livelihoods, and rural communities are connected. These experiences have given me a deeper appreciation for the people and systems that support rural India.

Agriculture, for me, is therefore not simply an interest. It is a part of my background and something I continue to learn about.

What I Do

Alongside my academics, I spend time exploring areas that interest me, including:

  • 🌱 Agriculture & Modern Farming
  • 💧 Water Conservation & Rural Development
  • 🚜 Agricultural Entrepreneurship
  • ☀️ Renewable Energy & Sustainability
  • 💡 Technology & Innovation
  • 🏘️ Community Development & Impact

I also enjoy writing and sharing my thoughts on topics related to agriculture, society, development, and contemporary issues.

MARCH

Model Action For Rural Change (MARCH) is an important part of my journey.

Established in 1997, MARCH has been involved in rural-development initiatives, including work related to watershed development and water-resource management.

Being connected with this work has helped me understand that rural development requires more than individual projects. It requires local participation, practical planning, responsible resource management, and long-term commitment.

Life Beyond Work

I am currently a student, and academics are an important part of my journey. Outside academics, tennis is one of my biggest interests.

Playing tennis has taught me discipline, consistency, patience, and the importance of continuously improving — lessons that I also carry into my other pursuits.

Always Learning

I am still at the beginning of my journey. I believe that being young is an opportunity to learn from people, explore different ideas, experience the real world, and turn curiosity into action.

This website is a space where I share my journey, interests, experiences, and work as I continue to learn and grow.

"Agriculture by roots. Athlete by discipline. Social impact by purpose."
